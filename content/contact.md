Questions, corrections and suggestions about Dart Tutorial are all welcome.

## How to reach me

Email **[santosh.ad215@gmail.com](mailto:santosh.ad215@gmail.com)**. Messages are usually answered within a few days.

## What to include

- **Found a mistake in a lesson?** Send the lesson's link and a short note about what is wrong. Code that does not run on the current Dart or Flutter version is especially useful to hear about.
- **Want a topic covered?** Say what you are trying to learn or build, and which lesson you were reading when you got stuck.
- **Anything else?** Business enquiries, partnerships and general feedback are welcome too.

## Privacy

What you send is used only to reply to you. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) for how this site handles data.
