Dart Tutorial teaches the Dart programming language and the Flutter framework, from the first line of code to a published app. There are two courses, [Dart](/#dart) and [Flutter](https://flutter.dart-tutorial.dev/), with more than 240 lessons between them. The Flutter course lives on its own site, [flutter.dart-tutorial.dev](https://flutter.dart-tutorial.dev/), and covers apps, every state management approach and game development with Flame. Every lesson follows the same shape: a short explanation, small programs you can run, and an exercise.

## Who writes it

The courses are created by **Santosh Adhikari**, CEO of SMAIT Technology, a software engineer and software architect. He teaches Dart and Flutter as **CoderSantoshAdhikari**, with free written lessons on this site and video lessons on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCo6suGrveQ0HFf46wfGBKXA). His goal is to make learning easy, practical and free for everyone, from the first line of code to published apps.

The lessons come from real projects: the questions beginners actually ask, and the mistakes that actually happen. Questions or corrections are welcome at [santosh.ad215@gmail.com](mailto:santosh.ad215@gmail.com).

## Learn in your language

The site has introductions in 12 languages: English, Hindi, Portuguese (Brazil), Indonesian, Vietnamese, German, Bengali, Urdu, Nepali, French, Japanese and Filipino. Every lesson can also be read translated with the globe button at the top of the page; the code stays the same in every language.

## How the lessons are written

- **In order.** Each lesson uses only what earlier lessons have covered.
- **Short.** One idea per lesson, usually five to ten minutes of reading.
- **Runnable.** Examples that can run in the browser have a Run button that opens them in DartPad.
- **Current.** The courses teach Dart 3 and current Flutter, and are revised as both change.
- **Plain.** No sign-up, no paywall, and progress tracking that stays in your browser.
